# Case study: Self-service learning ecosystem

> This case study is anonymized. Proprietary product details, internal systems, and customer information are omitted.

## Situation

A fast-release SaaS product had documentation, release briefs, training assets, and help content across multiple repositories. Customers and internal teams could find individual articles but lacked a clear learning journey.

## Objectives

- Reduce repeated support questions.
- Give each audience a clear path from first use to independent operation.
- Ensure training and documentation changed with weekly releases.
- Establish accountable content owners and review schedules.

## Actions

1. Audited 300+ articles and guides for audience, task, freshness, and duplication.
2. Grouped top tasks into role-based learning paths for administrators, customer-success teams, and partners.
3. Combined concise documentation with e-learning, videos, live sessions, and assessments.
4. Added release-readiness checkpoints so product changes triggered documentation and learning updates.
5. Defined governance for owners, technical review, editorial review, metadata, and retirement.
6. Used engagement and support themes to prioritize improvements.

## Result

The redesigned self-service ecosystem contributed to an approximately 30% reduction in inbound support dependency while improving content consistency and readiness across more than 100 consecutive weekly releases.

## What I would improve next

- Connect course and article events to customer-health reporting.
- Add role-based certification renewal.
- Build a search-failure dashboard and content-gap backlog.
- Use AI-assisted content audits with mandatory human validation.
