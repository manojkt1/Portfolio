# Measurement plan

## Adoption

- Enrollment and activation rate
- Course completion by role
- Time to first completed learning path
- Repeat usage of reference content

## Proficiency

- Assessment pass rate
- Most-missed questions
- Practical exercise completion
- Certification and renewal rate

## Product and support outcomes

- Support tickets by trained vs. untrained cohort
- Search queries with no useful result
- Article helpfulness and task completion
- Feature adoption after release education
- Customer-success escalations tied to knowledge gaps

## Content operations

- Content freshness
- Review SLA compliance
- Release documentation readiness
- Duplicate or retired content
- Technical-review turnaround time

Metrics should guide investigation, not replace user research or customer conversations.
