Self-Service Learning Ecosystem

How a fragmented set of product resources was reorganized into governed learning paths, release-ready enablement, and measurable self-service support.

~30%lower inbound support demand
100+weekly releases supported
300+articles governed
3audience learning paths

Challenge

Content was distributed across documentation, training, and release channels. Users struggled to find the right learning sequence, while support and customer-success teams repeated explanations.

Audience

Customer administrators, customer-success managers, and implementation or partner teams required different depth, sequencing, and proof of proficiency.

Approach

Audit content, map top tasks, define role-based paths, combine written and multimedia content, establish owners and review cycles, and connect releases to learning updates.

Measurement

Track search behavior, course completion, assessment performance, article use, support themes, freshness, and certification progress.
