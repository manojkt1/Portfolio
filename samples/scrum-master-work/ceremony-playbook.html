Agile Facilitation

Scrum Ceremony Playbook

How each ceremony was scoped, time-boxed, and run for a documentation and enablement team — small enough to stay lightweight, disciplined enough to stay predictable.

Daily Stand-up

1 Cadence

15 minutes, every working day, same time.

2 Focus

Yesterday's progress, today's plan, and active blockers — nothing else.

3 Artifact

Sprint board updated live; blockers logged and owned before the call ends.

Sprint Planning

1 Cadence

Start of every sprint, aligned to the release calendar.

2 Focus

Pull groomed backlog items, size them, and confirm capacity against release dates.

3 Artifact

Committed sprint goal and scoped backlog visible to engineering and product.

Backlog Grooming

1 Cadence

Mid-sprint, recurring, ahead of the next planning session.

2 Focus

Refine, estimate, split, and re-prioritize backlog items before they reach planning.

3 Artifact

A backlog with no stale, duplicate, or unestimated items at the top.

Retrospective

1 Cadence

End of every sprint, before planning the next one.

2 Focus

Blameless review of what helped, what hurt, and what to change.

3 Artifact

Action items with a named owner, tracked to closure the following sprint.
