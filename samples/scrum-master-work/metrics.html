Agile Governance

Sprint Metrics & Velocity Tracking

How sprint health was measured for a documentation and enablement team, to keep commitments honest without turning metrics into a performance-management tool.

Metric Framework

Metric Area Specific KPI Measurement Method Target Outcome
Sprint Predictability Committed vs. completed story points Sprint board burndown review Within 90–110% of commitment
Release Readiness Docs shipped on release day vs. total releases Release checklist audit 100% day-one readiness
Backlog Health % of top-of-backlog items groomed and estimated Backlog grooming review 100% groomed before planning
Retrospective Follow-through Action items closed by next retro Action-item tracker > 80% closed on time

Operational Feedback Loop

Velocity and predictability were reviewed at the start of every sprint planning session, not as a target to hit, but as a signal for whether estimation, scope, or dependencies needed adjusting. Retrospective action items were revisited at the next retrospective before new ones were added, so the backlog of process fixes never grew faster than the team's capacity to act on them.
